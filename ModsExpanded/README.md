# Mods Expanded

Adds 17 item mods in the same style as the base game: melee weapon, gun, bow, tool, armor and vehicle mods. Like vanilla mods they come in quality 1-6 (vehicle mods have no quality, like vanilla's), are found in loot and sold by traders, and have schematics that unlock them at the workbench.

They only use vanilla content, so they also fit weapons and tools from other mods that use the vanilla tags.

Adds new icons so has to be installed on server and client. `ModsExpanded-SERVERSIDE` is the server-only version: it uses tinted vanilla icons, so players don't need to install anything.

## Mods

| Name | Fits | Effect (quality 1 to 6) |
|---|---|---|
| Executioner | Melee | +10% to +35% damage to anything below 30% health |
| Live Wire | Melee | 5% to 15% chance a hit shocks the target and arcs to zombies within 3 m |
| Second Wind | Melee | Every kill gives back 5 to 20 stamina |
| Momentum | Melee | Each hit within 2 seconds of the last adds +5% melee damage, stacking 2 to 6 times |
| Headhunter | Guns, bows | +10% to +35% headshot damage |
| Speed Loader | Guns | Reloads 8% to 30% faster |
| Knock 'Em Down | Guns, bows | 3% to 15% chance a hit knocks the zombie down. Shotguns roll for each pellet at 1/8 of the chance |
| Spotter Tags | Guns, bows | Whatever you hit is marked through walls for every player for 4 to 12 seconds |
| Rabbit's Foot | Weapons, tools | Zombies you kill are 10% to 40% more likely to drop a loot bag |
| Turbo Motor | Chainsaw, auger | Runs 8% to 25% faster |
| Pay Dirt | Mining tools | +5% to +25% ore, stone and wood |
| Scrap Magnet | Salvage tools | +5% to +25% salvage |
| Thorns | Chest armor | Zombies and animals that hit you take 5 to 20 damage |
| Reactive Plating | Chest armor | Blocks the next hit you take, with a clang. Recharges in 60 to 30 seconds |
| Squad Comms | Helmets | You and party members within about 25 m deal +3% to +10% damage. Several radios don't stack |
| Off-Road Suspension | Vehicles | Your vehicle takes 40% less damage from collisions and has 20% more pulling power |
| Roadkill Bumper | Vehicles | Zombies you run over take 75% more damage. Doesn't take up the plow's slot |

## Where to find them

The mods are added to the vanilla mod loot groups for their tier, so they turn up wherever vanilla mods do, and traders stock them with the vanilla mods. Their schematics drop and sell alongside the vanilla mod schematics, each about as often as a vanilla one.
