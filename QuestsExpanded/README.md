# Changes

1. Trader quests will reset 2 days instead of every 3 days

2. Adds 120+ quests, offered by every trader: clear and fetch quests at urban and rural POIs (normal and infested), plus the commissions and defense quests below

3. Adds Commissions: crafting orders from the traders (see below)

4. Adds defense quests: Clear and Defend, Bounty Hunt, Build and Defend, Clear, Build and Defend, Last Stand, Blood Moon Bounty and Create Outpost (see below)

5. Adds NPC quests when you have NPC mods installed: Bandit Hideout, Rescue and Safehouse, plus bandit raids on outposts (see below). Without NPC mods they never show up

The mod used to replace the starting quest chain with a quest that just marks the trader. V3.4's own starting quest already does that, so the replacement was removed.

**Back up your save before adding or removing this mod.** Quest mods add C# code and quests that get saved with your character, so removing the mod from an existing save can reset your character to level 1.

## Removed: POI-group quests and the ZZTong version

The quests tied to specific groups of POIs (cabins, caves, gas stations, farms, stores, ...) and the old Clear and Defend quests are gone. The new quest types replace them: the urban and rural quests pick any POI with the right tags, so they also work with POI packs like ZZTong's, and Clear and Defend is back on the new wave system. Because of that, ZZZTongPrefabs-QuestsExpanded, which only existed to add ZZTong's POI names to those quests, is gone too; use QuestsExpanded with ZZTong's POIs instead.

Updating an existing save is safe: a removed quest you had in progress is dropped when the save loads (the log says `Quest with ID ... not found, ignoring`).

## V3.4 Updates

- Bumped to version 2.0.0.0
- Renamed `Localization.txt` to `Localization.csv` (the game no longer loads `.txt` localization from mods) and converted it to the V3.4 column layout. Quest text that contained commas or an unclosed quote is no longer cut off.
- Quests are added to each trader's own quest list (V3.4 replaced the shared `trader_quests` list)
- Rally points use V3.4's quest-type rally markers and quests carry `filter_tags="poi"` so the "POI quests" game option applies to them
- Reward loot groups renamed in V3.4 are remapped (`groupQuestSteel` -> `groupQuestSteel01`/`02` by tier, `groupQuestResources` -> `groupQuestResourcesT0`, T0 armor groups -> `groupQuestArmorT1`)
- POI vending machines are no longer slowed to a 2 day reset (V3.4 resets them daily)
- `QuestsExpanded.dll` rebuilt against V3.4 (see below)

## Fixes for reported bugs

- Fetch quests no longer pick POIs that have no spot for the satchel. The satchel used to spawn in another POI kilometers away, and leaving the quest area to get it failed the quest. Quests now use the same POI checks as vanilla: the POI must support the quest type (fetch, clear, infested), have sleepers, and not be in use by another player's quest.
- POI-specific quests (cabins, caves, gas stations, farms, ...) locked in one random POI name for the whole game session, so if that POI wasn't on your map the quest had no marker. They have been replaced (see above).
- In multiplayer, urban and rural quests taken by a client now search for POIs near the trader instead of near the corner of the map.
- Quest POIs show their proper names (e.g. "Crack-A-Book") instead of file names like `store_book_01`.
- The old defense quests could get stuck on a wave that never finished; the new wave system always ends its waves (see Defense quests).

## Commissions

A commission is a crafting order: the trader asks for a set of items you have to craft yourself, then you bring them back. Only items crafted after accepting the commission count, so loot and stockpiles don't help.

- Every trader offers generic orders (campfire rations, forge orders, chemistry orders, concrete, ammunition) and occasionally one that matches their specialty: Jen medical supplies, Hugh specialty rounds, Rekt hearty meals, Bob tools, Joel armor. 43 commissions across tiers 1-6.
- A trader only offers a commission when you have unlocked the recipes for everything in it, and offers at most one at a time.
- Rewards are the same as a fetch quest of the same tier, plus crafting magazines for that kind of crafting (and sometimes a workstation tool) as reward choices that are always offered.
- In a party, everyone's crafting counts toward a shared commission. Only the player who took it from the trader hands over the items; everyone turns it in for their own reward.

The commissions are generated by `QuestMaker\commissions.py` (`python commissions.py`), which rewrites the commissions section of `Config\quests.xml` and their localization rows. Edit the `ORDERS` list there to change or add orders.

## Defense quests

50 quests built on a new wave system. The waves follow your gamestage, each one bigger than the last, and always end. Every zombie that dies in the area during a wave counts toward it, however it died (traps, turrets, bleeding, other players) and whether it came with the wave or was drawn in by the noise; a wave's zombies that are still alive when the count runs out carry over into the next wave. A wave that runs 4 minutes sends whatever is left (stuck or lost zombies) away. The last few zombies of a wave show on the map and compass. There are 15 seconds between waves, with on-screen warnings and the wave and zombies left in the quest log. Leaving the area while the zombies are coming fails the quest.

- **Clear and Defend** (tiers 1-6, infested variant at tiers 3-6): clear a POI, then hold it against 3-5 waves. About half of each wave comes back out of the building you just cleared and the rest from just outside it, so the fight comes to you. The last wave brings a boss (at tier 1, a tougher Spider, Lumberjack or Fat Hawaiian). In the quests where you fortify a POI, the zombies come from just outside it instead, so your defenses matter.
- **Bounty Hunt** (tiers 2-6): a bounty boss is holed up in a POI with a few escorts. Clear the POI and kill it.
- **Horde Bounty** (tiers 2-6): go to a spot in the wild and wipe out a themed horde (cops, soldiers, spiders, bikers, burnt, lumberjacks, utility workers or zombie dogs) and the boss leading it.
- **Build and Defend** (tiers 1-5): the trader gives you a supply kit and a White River flag. Plant the flag at the marked site, then you have 5 minutes to build defenses around it before 3-6 waves come at it (with a boss at tiers 4-5). You can leave to gather more supplies during the build.
- **Clear, Build and Defend** (tiers 3-6, infested variant): the finale. Take the trader's supply kit, clear a POI, fortify it for 5 minutes, then hold it against a tougher horde with a boss in the last wave.
- **Last Stand** (tiers 1-6): plant the flag, build for 5 minutes, then hold against endless waves that keep getting harder, with a boss every 5th wave. During the 30-second breaks you can walk away to bank your pay for every wave held, and each wave pays more than the last. Flee during a wave or die and you get nothing. In a party everyone banks for themselves.
- **Blood Moon Bounty** (tiers 1-6, only offered in the 2 days before a blood moon): when the blood moon rises, bosses come for you wherever you are. At tiers 5-6 a new wave of bosses arrives every hour of the night. Kill them all without dying; there's no deadline.
- **Create Outpost** (tiers 2-6): clear a POI, hang the Duke's banner inside, and hold it for 2-5 nights. An attack hits every night at 22:00, bigger each night, with warnings before it. Leave during the day if you like, but someone has to be there when the attack comes, and the banner has to survive.

Bosses are tougher, bigger versions of their zombie type with a health bar overhead and a marker on the map and compass, and always drop a boss loot bag: Biker or Fat Cop at tier 2, Feral Wight or Demolisher at tier 3, Radiated Mutated or Radiated Screamer at tier 4, a Charged variant at tier 5 and an Infernal variant at tier 6.

In a party everyone sharing the quest fights the same waves (one set of zombies, sized to the party) and everyone's quest completes together. Each member gets their own supply kit, and the flag one member plants counts for the whole party.

## NPC quests (only with NPC mods)

15 quests with human NPCs: bandits to fight and survivors to rescue. The base game has no NPCs that can do this, so these quests use the NPCs other mods add. QuestsExpanded doesn't need any of them: traders only offer an NPC quest when the NPCs it needs are installed, and without NPC mods nothing changes.

- **What to install:** [0-SCore](https://www.nexusmods.com/7daystodie/mods/6176) and [NPC Core](https://www.nexusmods.com/7daystodie/mods/8099) (on the server and every client, like any NPC mod). NPC Core comes with bandits and friendly NPCs of its own; NPC packs (bandits, soldiers, civilians) add more variety, and the quests use them automatically.
- **How it finds them:** when the game starts, QuestsExpanded sorts every installed creature by the base game's own NPC types and factions: hostile humans (bandits) and friendly NPCs that can follow a player (made with SCore). It never links to SCore itself.

The quests:
- **Bandit Hideout** (tiers 2-6): bandits have taken over a POI. Kill every one of them; from tier 3 a bandit leader with the boss buff holds the far end and drops a boss loot bag. The POI's zombies are there as usual but don't have to be cleared, and the gunfire draws them in against both sides. Low tiers get the weaker bandits (by weapon), tier 6 any of them.
- **Rescue** (tiers 2-6): a group of 3-6 survivors is trapped at the far end of a POI and under attack when you arrive: zombies besieging them, bandits holding them, or at tiers 5-6 both. Break the attack, then walk up to each survivor and they follow you (they keep up, ride along in vehicles and catch up if they fall behind). Lead them to the trader; more attackers come when the group starts moving and again once it's out of the POI's area. You're paid more for every survivor who makes it, and the quest only fails if they all die. Survivors can't be hurt until a player gets close to them. Hand them weapons, armor or medicine (open their inventory once they follow you) and they use it. At tier 4 and up, each player can pick "a survivor joins you" as a reward and get one of them as a hired companion.
- **Safehouse** (tiers 2-6): the trader gives you a safehouse flag. Hang it wherever you want and build the refugees a home around it: within 10 blocks of the flag you need a bedroll under a roof for each refugee, a campfire, a light, a storage box and a door; tier 3 adds a workbench, tier 4 a dew collector, tier 5 a chemistry station and tier 6 an electric light with power. The quest log shows what's still missing. Then 2-5 refugees wait just outside the trader's compound: lead them home. Once they've all moved in, the safehouse is attacked that night at 22:00 (3 waves); someone has to be there, and the refugees fight alongside you. Win with at least one refugee alive, and they stay and guard the safehouse.
- **Bandit raids** (Create Outpost, tiers 3-6): one night of each outpost (never the first) is a bandit raid instead of zombies.

In a party everyone sharing the quest fights the same bandits and leads the same group; each survivor or refugee follows whoever walks up to them, and waits where they are if that player dies or leaves. Once the group is following you, dying doesn't fail a Rescue. A Safehouse is kept with the world like an outpost, so it survives logouts and restarts.

The NPC quests are generated by `QuestMaker\npc.py` (`python npc.py`, after `defense.py`), which rewrites the NPC section of `Config\quests.xml`, its part of `Config\gameevents.xml` and their localization rows.

## Generating the defense quests

The defense quests are generated by `QuestMaker\defense.py` (`python defense.py`), which rewrites the defense section of `Config\quests.xml`, the supply kits in `Config\gameevents.xml` and their localization rows. Waves, bosses, kits and rewards are tables at the top of the script. `QuestMaker\config.py` writes the bosses' buffs and the quest flags.

## Building QuestsExpanded.dll

The custom quest objectives (`RandomTaggedPOIGotoDEM`, `DeliverDEM`, `DefendWavesDEM`, `PlaceFlagDEM`, `BuildPhaseDEM`, `LastStandDEM`, `BloodMoonBountyDEM`, `OutpostDEM`, `BanditHideoutDEM`, `RescueDEM`, `SafehouseDEM`), the Last Stand and Rescue rewards, the defense wave director, the NPC quests' NPC finder, the quest flag block and the commission patches live in `src`. `RandomTaggedPOIGotoDEM` is adapted from SCore's `RandomTaggedPOIGotoSDX`. They only reference the game's own DLLs, so SCore does not need to be installed; the NPC quests reach SCore by name when it is.

Run `src\build.bat`. It compiles against the installed game with the C# compiler that ships with Visual Studio (no .NET SDK needed).
